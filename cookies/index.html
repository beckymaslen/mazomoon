Cookies

Last updated 5 November 2026

mazomoon.com only uses analytics cookies if you say yes. We never use advertising cookies.

Analytics cookies (only if you accept)

We use Google Analytics to see which pages people visit, how they found us and which buttons they tap, so we can make the site better. It sets these cookies:

  • _ga: tells one visit from another. Lasts 2 years.
  • _ga_5131GXSM7M: keeps track of your current visit. Lasts 2 years.

If you decline, Google Analytics doesn't load at all and these cookies aren't set. If you change your mind later, we delete them.

What the site stores without asking

  • Your cookie choice: saved in your browser's local storage so we don't ask on every page.
  • Session storage: the site remembers, for the current visit only, that you've already seen the opening animation, so it doesn't play again on every page. This is cleared when you close the tab.

Other websites

Our fonts come from Google Fonts, and some product photos load from Amazon. When you tap "Buy on Amazon", you move to Amazon's website, which uses its own cookies under Amazon's cookie notice.

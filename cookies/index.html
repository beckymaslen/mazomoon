Cookies

Last updated 4 November 2026

mazomoon.com doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't run analytics that follow you around the web.

What the site does store

  • Session storage: the site remembers, for the current visit only, that you've already seen the opening animation, so it doesn't play again on every page. This is cleared when you close the tab.

Other websites

Our fonts come from Google Fonts, and some product photos load from Amazon. When you tap "Buy on Amazon", you move to Amazon's website, which uses its own cookies under Amazon's cookie notice.

If we ever add analytics or anything else that uses cookies, we'll update this page and ask for your consent first where the law requires it.

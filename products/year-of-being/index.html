Sound familiar?

Each year goes by faster than the last

Their favourite things change every few months. Best friends, worries, the thing they want to be when they grow up. And you never quite notice the moment it changes.

School reports and photos keep a record, but not of what it actually felt like to be them that year.

A gentler way

Let them write their own record of the year

Each journal is written for one age, with questions that fit where they are now. They fill it in through the year, drawing and writing about feelings, school, friends and ordinary days.

Collect one each birthday, and you’ll end up with a shelf of who they were at 7, 8, 9 and beyond.

What it is

One journal for each age

The same calm style for every year, with themes that grow with them.

Questions

Things people ask me

Which age should I buy?

Buy the age they are now, or the age they’re turning if it’s a birthday present. The journal is meant to be filled in over that year.

Are the journals different for each age?

Yes. Each age has its own theme and questions, from feelings and confidence at 7 to voice and independence at 12, all in the same style.

Paperback or hardcover?

The pages are the same. Hardcover is sturdier and lasts better on a shelf, and paperback is lighter and a little cheaper.

Can they fill it in on their own?

Most children from seven can, with a bit of help spelling now and then. Older children often prefer to keep it private.

What if they start partway through the year?

That’s fine. Nothing is dated, so they can start any time and fill it in at their own pace.

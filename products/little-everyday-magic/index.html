Sound familiar?

Everyday life can feel a bit ordinary

Same commute, same washing, same dinner. Some weeks it’s hard to notice anything lovely at all.

And winding down at the end of the day often means a screen, which never quite does the job.

A gentler way

Look closer. There’s magic in the detail.

Each page is a cosy corner of a village: a railway halt, a midnight bakery, a bicycle by the gate, a beekeeper’s garden, a knitting corner. And in every one, something small is misbehaving: a luggage tag floating off, a loaf rising a little too high.

Colour slowly, find the magic, and let the day go.

Questions

Things people ask me

When is it out?

Soon. Leave your email on this page and I’ll tell you the day it’s on Amazon.

What is the “magic” in each picture?

Something small and gentle that isn’t quite ordinary, like a loaf rising too high or a tag floating away. Finding it is part of the fun.

Is it for adults?

Yes, it’s designed for adults, with fine detail. Older children who love detailed colouring may enjoy it too.

What should I colour with?

Colouring pencils give the softest results. Gel pens and fine liners are lovely for small details.

How is it different from my other colouring books?

A Cup of Somewhere Else is cosy places to escape to, and Just One More Chapter is all about books. This one is village life with a touch of magic.

Sound familiar?

This year will go faster than you think

Eight is a year of growing up a little at a time: more independence, stronger opinions, and learning to trust themselves.

Their favourite things change every few months, and the photos and school reports don’t show what it actually felt like to be them.

A gentler way

Let them write their own record of the year

The Year of Being 8 gives them a private place to write and draw about themselves: what they’re good at, who matters to them, what they love doing, and what they hope for.

Some days they’ll have lots to say, and other days just a little. The journal tells them both are perfectly fine.

What it is

Questions

Things people ask me

Is it right for my eight-year-old?

It’s written for children who are eight, or turning eight. If they’re between ages, choose the year they’re about to start, as they’ll fill it in over the coming months.

Will they need help filling it in?

Most children this age can use it on their own, with help spelling now and then. Many pages can be drawn rather than written.

Paperback or hardcover?

The pages are the same. Hardcover is sturdier and lasts better on a shelf, and paperback is lighter and a little cheaper.

Is it a good birthday present?

Yes. It’s a thoughtful present for a eightth birthday, and lovely to give alongside a set of pens.

What if they start partway through the year?

That’s fine. Nothing is dated, so they can start any time and fill it in at their own pace.

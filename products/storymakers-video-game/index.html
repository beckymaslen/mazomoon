Questions

What age is it for?

Roughly six to twelve. Younger children love the heroes and pixel art, and older ones will enjoy planning levels and bosses.

Do they need a computer or console?

No. Everything happens on paper with a pencil. No screens needed.

Do they need to be good at drawing?

No. Pixel grids make characters easy, and stick figures are fine for everything else.

How is it different from The Storymaker’s Comic Book?

The Comic Book is for telling stories in panels. This one is about designing whole video games, from the hero to the box art.

Questions

Things people ask me

Are they really waterproof?

Yes. They’re waterproof vinyl with a protective laminate, so they’re fine on water bottles and cups. For the longest life, hand wash rather than putting them through a hot dishwasher every day.

Will they peel off my laptop?

They have a strong adhesive designed to stay put on curves, glass and metal. Press firmly from the middle outwards when you stick them on.

Are there any duplicates?

No. There are 50 stickers and 50 different designs.

How big are they?

Each sticker is about 5 to 6 cm, roughly the width of a bank card’s short side, so they fit planners and phone cases.

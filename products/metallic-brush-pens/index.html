Questions

Things people ask me

Do they work on dark paper?

Metallic ink tends to look its best on dark or kraft paper and card, where the shine really stands out. They also work on white.

Are they good for beginners?

Yes. A brush tip makes thick and thin strokes easy. Try pressing harder on the downstrokes and lighter on the upstrokes.

How long do they take to dry?

Give them a moment longer than a normal pen, as metallic ink sits on top of the paper.

Can children use them?

They’re water-based and low odour, but they’re designed for older children and adults rather than little ones.

What if one doesn’t write?

A new brush pen sometimes needs a few strokes on scrap paper. If it still won’t write, Amazon’s returns process covers it, or email me at hello@mazomoon.com.

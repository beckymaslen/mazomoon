Questions

Things people ask me

Do they bleed through paper?

On most journal and planner paper they don’t. On very thin paper, any gel ink can show through a little, so it’s worth testing on the back page first.

Are they good for left-handed writers?

Gel ink takes a moment to dry, so left-handers may want to give each line a second before moving their hand over it.

How do I know when they’re running out?

The barrels are clear, so you can see the ink level at a glance.

Are they refillable?

No, they’re designed to be used until the ink runs out.

What if one doesn’t write?

Occasionally a new gel pen needs a few scribbles to get going. If one still won’t write, Amazon’s returns process covers it, or email me at hello@mazomoon.com.

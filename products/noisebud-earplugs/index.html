Questions

Things people ask me

Will I still hear my alarm?

They’re designed to soften noise rather than block it, so most people still hear alarms and voices. If you rely on an alarm, test them on a weekend first.

Can I sleep on my side in them?

Yes. They’re soft silicone, and many people find them comfortable on their side.

How do I clean them?

Rinse with mild soap and warm water, then let them dry fully before putting them back in.

How do I find the right fit?

Several tip sizes are included. Start with the middle size and swap until they feel snug but comfortable, with no pressure.

Are they a medical hearing protector?

No. They’re for everyday comfort, like sleep, focus and travel. For concerts, power tools or work noise, use rated hearing protection.

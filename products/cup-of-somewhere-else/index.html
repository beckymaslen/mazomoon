A gentler way

Go somewhere else for a while

Each page is a little place to escape to: a cabin in the Highlands, a canal boat at dawn, an attic above the rooftops of Paris. In every one, there’s a cup waiting for you.

Pick a place that suits your mood: a simpler scene to unwind, or a fuller one when you want to get lost in the detail. Make the walls any colour you like. Add rain, or sunshine. Leave the kettle on.

Questions

Things people ask me

Can I use pens or markers?

Yes. Each picture is printed on its own page, so ink won’t spoil another design. If you use markers, slip a spare sheet of paper behind the page you’re colouring.

Is it suitable for beginners?

Yes. There’s a mix of simpler scenes and fuller, more detailed ones, so you can choose whatever suits your mood and confidence.

Is it for adults or children?

It’s designed for adults, with fine detail. Older children who enjoy detailed colouring may like it too.

What should I colour with?

Colouring pencils give the softest results. Gel pens and fine liners work well for small details.

Is it a good gift?

It’s a lovely gift for someone who needs a breather: a new parent, a busy friend, or anyone who loves cosy things.

Questions

Things people ask me

What age is it for?

Roughly six to twelve. Younger children can draw the pictures and dictate the words, and older ones will enjoy planning proper stories.

What should they draw with?

Pencil first, then pens or colouring pencils. The pages are white so colours look bright. Heavy markers may show through.

Do they need to be good at drawing?

No. The book itself says stick figures are absolutely fine. Clear is better than perfect.

How is it different from The Storymaker’s Journal?

This one is all comics, on big A4 pages. The Journal is smaller and covers characters, worlds and story ideas, with a little comic making.

Is it good for two children to share?

With twelve comics there’s room to take turns, but most children like having their own book with their name on the certificate.

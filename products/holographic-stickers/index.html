Questions

Things people ask me

Are they see-through?

Yes, the base is clear, so the surface underneath shows through and only the foil design stands out.

Are they waterproof?

Yes. They’re waterproof PET vinyl, so they’re fine on bottles and phone cases.

What colour are they?

The foil is holographic, so it shifts through every colour depending on the light. On a dark surface they look especially bright.

Do they show up on white paper?

Yes, the foil shows on white, but the colour shift looks strongest on darker or kraft pages.

Questions

What age is it for?

Roughly six to twelve. Younger children can design the badges and kits, and older ones will enjoy the formations, tactics and the full season.

Do they need dice?

Yes, one ordinary dice for the football season. Any dice from a board game will do.

Do they need to be good at drawing?

No. Kits, badges and stadiums can be as simple or as detailed as they like.

How is it different from The Storymaker’s Comic Book?

The Comic Book is for telling stories in panels. This one is all about building and managing football clubs.

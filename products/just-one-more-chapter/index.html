Sound familiar?

Some nights your eyes are too tired to read

You want the cosy feeling of a good book, but it’s late, the words are swimming, and the phone is right there.

Scrolling never gives you the same calm as turning a page.

A gentler way

Colour the places books take you

Every page is a bookish little world: a bookshop in a storm, a teetering to-be-read tower, a little free library at the end of a lane, a blanket fort with a sleeping dog.

It’s the same slow, calm feeling as reading, for the nights when you’d rather hold a pencil.

Questions

Things people ask me

When is it out?

Soon. Leave your email on this page and I’ll tell you the day it’s on Amazon.

Is it like A Cup of Somewhere Else?

It’s in the same cosy style, but every scene is about books and reading. A Cup of Somewhere Else is about places to escape to.

Is it for adults?

Yes, it’s designed for adults, with fine detail. Older children who love detailed colouring may enjoy it too.

What should I colour with?

Colouring pencils give the softest results. Gel pens and fine liners are lovely for small details.

Will it be a good gift?

For a book lover, absolutely. It pairs nicely with a reading light or a book.

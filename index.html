Review ✦ Sample post

The book I read twice in one wet October.

12 October 20266 min readBy Becky

This is a sample review to show how a post will look. Your own words go here, written the way you'd tell a friend about a book over coffee.

Start with how you found it. Maybe it was a charity shop find, a recommendation from your sister, or the cover that caught your eye in the library. Then tell us how it made you feel, rather than retelling the plot.

"Some books you read. Some books you live in for a week."

Pull out a favourite line or moment, and say who you think would love it. Keep paragraphs short and leave plenty of white space, so it reads like a calm evening rather than homework.

Finish with your cosy rating and what you're reading next. If a Mazo Moon product genuinely helped (a reading light, a journal for notes), mention it naturally at the end.

Our Story & Contact

A little Yorkshire heart in your everyday.

We're Mazo Moon, a Yorkshire family business with a soft spot for big imaginations and life's little details. I'm Rebecca, and I started it as a mum who wanted to keep hold of the little moments.

It started with a stack of half-used notebooks and a wish for something calmer. We wanted journals that didn't shout at you to be productive, colouring books you could pick up for ten minutes, and stationery that made the page feel like a nice place to be.

Today we design everything ourselves and send it out through Amazon, so it can reach you quickly wherever you are in the UK. We read every review, and plenty of our favourite ideas have come from customers.

This space is where we share the rest of it: the books we're reading, the small things we love, and what's coming next.

"I make journals for the things you swear you'll remember, then forget by teatime."

Rebecca, founder
